SECTION 4 — Danh mục 10 metrics và hướng diễn giải
====================================================================================================
+--------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------+
| Metric                   | Định nghĩa                                                                      | Orientation trong paired comparison |
+==========================+=================================================================================+=====================================+
| roc_auc_approved         | ROC-AUC, positive = Approved, score p_approved                                  | higher                              |
| average_precision_denied | Average Precision (PR-AUC), positive = Denied, score p_denied                   | higher                              |
| precision_denied         | TN/(TN+FN) tại threshold                                                        | higher                              |
| recall_denied            | TN/(TN+FP) tại threshold                                                        | higher                              |
| f1_denied                | 2TN/(2TN+FN+FP)                                                                 | higher                              |
| recall_approved          | TP/(TP+FN)                                                                      | higher                              |
| balanced_accuracy        | (Recall Approved + Recall Denied)/2                                             | higher                              |
| brier_score_approved     | mean((p_approved − y)^2)                                                        | lower                               |
| calibration_intercept    | shift a: mean(expit(logit(p)+a)) = mean(y) (calibration-in-the-large; target 0) | |x−0| lower                         |
| calibration_slope        | slope b của logistic recalibration y ~ a + b·logit(p) (target 1)                | |x−1| lower                         |
+--------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------+
Score-based: ROC-AUC, AP Denied, Brier, calibration intercept/slope (không phụ thuộc threshold). Decision-based: precision/recall/F1 Denied,
Recall Approved, BA (phụ thuộc threshold). logit dùng clip p vào [1e-15, 1−1e-15].
